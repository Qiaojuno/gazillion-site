Gazillion Support

Gazillion is a word game where rare answers fly higher. Each prompt asks you to name something, and the rarer your answer, the higher your bird climbs.

Frequently asked questions

The game says it can't connect

Gazillion needs an internet connection: the prompts and their answer lists live online so they can keep growing. Check that you're online, then try again from Home.

How does scoring work?

Every answer on a prompt's list lifts your bird. Common answers barely leave the ground; the rarer the answer, the higher you go, up to One in a Gazillion. An answer that isn't on the list gets No Lift. If you're a letter or two off, the game asks "Did you mean…?" so a typo doesn't cost you.

When does the Daily change?

Every day at midnight, your local time. Everyone gets the same seven prompts, and you get one flight a day. If you leave a Daily part-way, you can return to it from Home (RETURN TO FLIGHT); the prompt you left on counts as a miss.

Who am I playing in 1 vs 1?

1 vs 1 uses Game Center. FIND A RIVAL looks for another player; if nobody is searching after a few seconds, a computer stand-in plays instead, answering the way a typical player would. To play a friend, one of you taps CREATE CODE and the other enters the 4-digit code. Codes always wait for a real friend.

My answer should have counted!

On the results screen, tap That should count! next to the refused answer. Reports are read and the answer lists are updated online, with no app update needed.

How do I keep my progress on a new device?

Sign in to iCloud on both devices. Your bests, feathers and birds sync through your own private iCloud storage.

How do I get new birds?

You earn one feather for every 10 m you climb. Tap your feathers on Home to open the shop and spend them on new birds. There are no in-app purchases: every bird is earned by playing.

Can I turn off the sound, music or vibration?

Yes. Use the speaker and gear buttons on Home.
