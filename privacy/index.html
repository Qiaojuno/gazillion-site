Gazillion Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026

Gazillion is a word game made by Nicholas Ho ("I", "me"). This policy explains what the game collects, why, and what control you have. The short version: Gazillion has no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and I never see who you are.

What Gazillion collects

Answers you report ("That should count!"). On the results screen you can report an answer you think the game should have accepted. A report contains the prompt, the answer as you typed it, and the spelling it was checked against. It is sent to Apple's CloudKit service, where only I can read it. It does not include your name, Apple Account, email address or location. CloudKit attaches an anonymous identifier to every record it stores; I cannot use it to find out who you are. Reports are used only to improve the answer lists. Players can't read each other's reports.

That is the only information that leaves your device for me.

What stays with you

Your progress. Your best altitudes, feathers, birds and past flights are saved on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, they are also kept in your own private iCloud storage so they carry over to your other devices. That storage belongs to your Apple Account: I cannot see or access it.

A Daily flight left part-way is kept on your device only, so you can return to it.

Settings such as sound, music and haptics are kept on your device only.

1 vs 1 and Game Center

1 vs 1 uses Apple's Game Center. To find a rival, Game Center uses your Game Center player profile under Apple's own terms and privacy policy. During a match, the two devices exchange your Game Center display name, the bird you're wearing and your answers, so each game can show the race. Nothing from the match is stored by me. When nobody is available, a computer-controlled stand-in plays instead.

What Gazillion does not do

  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites, and no data brokers.
  • No analytics or third-party SDKs.
  • No collection of your name, email address, contacts, photos, location or device identifiers.
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone.

Emails you send

If you use "Report a bug" in the game, your email app opens a message to me with the game's version, your device type (for example "iPhone") and its iOS version filled in. You choose whether to send it. I use what you send only to reply and fix the problem, and delete it when it's no longer needed.

Children

Gazillion is not directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone. The only thing the game sends me is an answer report, which contains no personal information.

Your choices

  • You can play without ever sending a report.
  • You can turn off iCloud for Gazillion in Settings > [your name] > iCloud, or delete its iCloud data in Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Account Storage.
  • Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device.
  • To ask about a report you sent, email me and I'll help.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
